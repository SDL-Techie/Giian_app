PRODUCTION APPROVAL ASSETS

The included signature.png and stamp.png are intentionally marked placeholders.
They are NOT valid company signature/seal assets and must not be used on customer-facing legal documents.

Production setup:
1. Replace signature.png with a genuine authorised company signature image.
2. Replace stamp.png with the genuine authorised company stamp/seal image.
3. Confirm the files are approved for use by the company.
4. Set COMPANY_APPROVAL_ASSETS_ENABLED=true in the production environment.
5. Restart the backend and generate a staging invoice to verify placement.

Until step 4, approved invoices show the system approval metadata only and do not render either image.
